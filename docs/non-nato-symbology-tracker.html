Non-NATO Symbology Rules Tracker

Working document, not a smoke test. The maintainer lays out non-NATO symbology rules category by category and domain by domain; each item here is one rule to specify, checked off once it has been stated. Items tagged SETTLED already have a rule recorded (see the note under each) — everything else is open. State is saved in this browser. See docs/non-nato-symbology-tracker.md for the same content as plain text.

Current scope (2026-08-26): Land, plus a Land-scoped slice of SIGINT (Jammer/Radar only) and a yet-to-be-picked subset of Control Measures. Every other domain has been reviewed against the catalog and marked not required outright — not deferred, genuinely out of scope for this work. Rules are being worked out against two reference documents kept locally, gitignored, in reference/non-nato/.
